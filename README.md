# IOB NRI Products

A single self-contained page presenting **Indian Overseas Bank's NRI products** —
accounts, the NRI Plus deposit scheme, savings tiers and remittance tracking.

Published with GitHub Pages: <https://pandianss.github.io/NRIProducts/>

## Contents

- `index.html` — the whole page. No build step and no external assets: fonts come
  from Google Fonts and every illustration is inline SVG.

## Sections

Hero · account chooser (three questions, branches to the right account type) ·
NRE, NRO, FCNR(B) and RFC explained · NRI Plus fixed deposit scheme with the
Rs. 1 crore illustration · side-by-side comparison of all four accounts ·
Elevate / Privilege / Signature savings tiers · remittance tracing ·
returning to India and the RFC path · FAQ · NRI help desk.

## Note

Reference material, not a contractual offer and not tax advice. All accounts are
governed by FEMA, Reserve Bank of India regulations and the bank's own terms.
Interest rates, charges, tier benefits and limits change from time to time and are
published separately by the bank. The NRI Plus yields shown are indicative scheme
rates and carry no date in the source material — confirm the rate applicable on the
day of booking.
